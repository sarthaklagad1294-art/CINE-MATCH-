The intuition

Words become coordinates.

Imagine every movie as a point in a very large space. A film about space, robots, and emotional survival lands near other films using similar language. The closer the direction, the stronger the recommendation.

Why TF-IDF?

Not all words carry the same signal.

“Movie” appears everywhere, so it should not decide a match. TF-IDF downweights common words and boosts distinctive terms such as “wormhole”, “samurai”, or “heist”.

For your viva

Three points worth remembering.

01

Content-based. CineMatch recommends from movie metadata, not from other users’ ratings.

02

Cached. The vectorizer and similarity matrix are saved with joblib, so startup stays fast.

03

Flexible. The same model accepts a title or a new free-text description.
